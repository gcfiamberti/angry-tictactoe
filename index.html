Instructions

Welcome

Thank you for taking part in this fun experience.
You will now play tic tac toe against the computer on a 3×3 grid.

You are X and the computer is O. Tap an empty square to place your symbol. The winner is the first player to complete three symbols in a row, column, or diagonal.

You will play 10 matches. You earn 1 point for each win and lose 1 point for each loss.

After the game you will answer two simple questions about how you felt playing this game.
